The Challenge

Modernize Without Breaking What Works

The existing intranet was outdated and fragmented. But the company had years of valuable knowledge stored in Confluence — content that couldn't be disrupted. The project needed to thread a needle: deliver a modern, branded experience while keeping all of that institutional knowledge intact.

Key requirements included:

  • A custom UI/UX reflecting the company's design ethos
  • A bespoke calendar and events module
  • Brand-aligned CSS and component design
  • Analytics tracking for search terms and user behavior

The Solution

A Tailored Confluence Experience

Rather than rebuilding from scratch, M20 built on Confluence's robust content management capabilities — layering on Refined for Confluence for theming, custom HTML components for functionality, and analytics for insight.

Design & Features

Built to Match the Brand

M20 worked closely with Refined for Confluence to deliver a pixel-perfect intranet that resonated with the client's design identity — without touching existing Confluence content.

Outcomes

A Platform That Earned C-Suite Attention

The revamped intranet launched to immediate internal acclaim — including recognition from the executive team. It proved that internal tools can reflect the same design standards as customer-facing products.
